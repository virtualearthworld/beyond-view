Measure what the camera actually saw.

This lab runs in your browser. It samples frames from your selected video, detects visual corners, tracks matching image patches, and measures apparent motion between views. The video is processed locally by this page; it is not uploaded.

Choose a video to begin. This is a real reconstruction-development diagnostic, not a completed 3D conversion.

Observed feature movement

The overlay shows measured image correspondences between two frames. Lines are observations from the video—not guessed objects or a 3D scene. More stable correspondences can help estimate camera movement, which is a required step before triangulation.

After analysis, the first sampled frame and tracked feature movements will appear here.

What this milestone proves—and what it does not

  • Implemented here: local frame sampling, corner detection, patch matching, and a visible measurement of image motion.
  • Next: robust camera-pose estimation, calibrated triangulation, multi-view consistency checks, and conversion of measured points into textured surfaces.
  • Not implemented yet: an end-to-end converter or a walkable 3D reconstruction. This page does not claim otherwise.
