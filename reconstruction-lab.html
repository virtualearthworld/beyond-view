Multi-frame consistency check

This compares each adjacent sampled pair across the clip. It reports feature matches, robust 2D-motion support, and perspective-model support separately. After linking persistent image tracks, the lab applies confidence-weighted smoothing to accumulated translation within each connected component. The experimental bundle-adjustment stage jointly refines sparse landmarks and camera translations while holding rotations and intrinsics fixed. The first camera in each component is anchored. This is not full bundle adjustment across rotations, intrinsics, and scale. The evaluator triangulates persistent tracks from their first and last observations, then checks reprojection in every observed frame, comparing raw and regularized translations using identical tracks and assumed intrinsics. Failed gaps are not bridged, and scale remains unknown.

What this milestone proves—and what it does not

  • Implemented here: local frame sampling at 0.5-second intervals (up to 48 frames), corner detection, patch matching, multi-frame feature trajectory linking, camera-pose quality diagnostics, and sparse triangulation checks. Weak-parallax points below the configured minimum angle are counted and excluded from the stable sparse cloud.
  • Experimental bundle adjustment: robust alternating reprojection refinement jointly updates sparse landmark positions and camera translations; rotations and intrinsics remain fixed, and each component has an anchored first camera. It is not metric-scale recovery or full bundle adjustment.
  • Multi-view validation: persistent tracks are triangulated from their first and last observations and reprojected into every observed frame. Raw and regularized translation paths are compared using the same tracks and assumed intrinsics; this is a diagnostic, not independent ground truth or bundle adjustment.
  • Next: validate bundle adjustment on held-out observations and independent camera calibration, then improve multi-view consistency and convert reliable measured geometry into textured surfaces.
  • Not implemented yet: an end-to-end converter or a walkable 3D reconstruction. This page does not claim otherwise.
