This compares each adjacent sampled pair across the clip. It reports feature matches, robust 2D-motion support, and perspective-model support separately. After linking contiguous tracks with at least 3 observations inside each connected camera component, the lab uses those tracks for confidence-weighted trajectory smoothing, experimental bundle adjustment, and multi-view reprojection checks. A stricter subset spanning every sampled frame is reported separately; optimization no longer depends on that unusually restrictive subset. Bundle adjustment still holds intrinsics fixed, anchors the first camera in each component, and does not recover metric scale. Reprojection triangulates each track from its first and last observations and checks its interior observations. Failed gaps are not bridged.

What this milestone proves—and what it does not

  • Implemented here: local frame sampling at 0.75-second intervals (up to 32 frames), corner detection, patch matching, multi-frame feature trajectory linking, camera-pose quality diagnostics, and sparse triangulation checks. Weak-parallax points below the configured minimum angle are counted and excluded from the stable sparse cloud.
  • Experimental bundle adjustment: contiguous epipolar-inlier tracks with at least 3 observations are used for translation smoothing and landmark/camera refinement. Rotations and intrinsics remain fixed, and each component has an anchored first camera. It is not metric-scale recovery or full bundle adjustment.
  • Multi-view validation: the same 3+ view track set is triangulated from first/last observations and checked against interior observations. Tracks spanning every sampled frame are reported separately as a stricter subset; they are not the sole optimization input. This is not independent camera-pose ground truth.
  • Next: validate bundle adjustment on held-out observations and independent camera calibration, then improve multi-view consistency and convert reliable measured geometry into textured surfaces.
  • Distributed support gate: a component must have at least 16 consistent tracks spread over 6 of 12 image cells, at least 3 columns and 2 rows, and adequate temporal coverage before its tracks can enter optimization, sparse geometry, fusion, or PLY export. This measures evidence coverage, not semantic identity.
  • Camera-path coverage: observed camera-center edges are summarized by connected component; failed pose gaps are not bridged. The X–Z plot is not gravity-aligned and does not define a walkable region.
  • Not implemented yet: an end-to-end converter or a walkable 3D reconstruction. This page does not claim otherwise.
