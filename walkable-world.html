Enter the 3D world

This test uses actual 3D geometry, so moving closer to a tree makes you approach the tree in the world instead of enlarging a flat picture.

Computer: click Enter, then use W A S D to walk and move the mouse to look around. Press Esc to release the mouse.

Touchscreen: use the arrow pad and drag on the scene to look around.

This proves the navigation/player layer only. Turning a real video into a detailed, freely walkable copy of its location is a separate reconstruction step and is not implemented by this demo.
