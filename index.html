Tips for recording a future 3D scene
  • Move slowly and keep the camera steady.
  • Capture overlapping views from several angles.
  • Include clear details and textured surfaces.
  • Avoid fast turns, heavy blur, and large jumps in viewpoint.

These tips can help reconstruction, but they do not guarantee that a video can produce a complete walkable 3D scene.
